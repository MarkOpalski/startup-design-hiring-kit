# Design Lead vs. Individual Contributor (IC)

Understanding the difference between these roles helps avoid mismatches in startup hiring.

---

## Design Lead

**Best for:** Seed-stage and beyond, when design maturity is growing.

### Traits

- Provides strategic direction and mentorship
- Establishes design processes and principles
- Advocates for the design team cross-functionally

### Risks

- May be too abstract or removed from shipping
- Needs a team to lead—can’t be a solo contributor forever

---

## Individual Contributor (IC)

**Best for:** Startups that need hands-on design work and fast iteration

### Traits

- Executes design work directly
- Collaborates closely with engineers and PMs
- Often more focused on output than process

### Risks

- Needs autonomy and clarity
- May lack systems-thinking or leadership experience

---

## Key Distinction

| Category | Design Lead         | IC Designer       |
| -------- | ------------------- | ----------------- |
| Focus    | Strategy, org, team | Execution, UX/UI  |
| Reports  | May manage others   | Usually solo      |
| Output   | Guidance, direction | Designs, flows    |
| Good for | Scaling teams       | MVPs, fast builds |
