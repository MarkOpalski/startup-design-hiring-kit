# Fractional Design Lead

**Best for:** Startups that need senior design leadership but can't hire full-time.

---

## What They Do

- Help define hiring plans and design org strategy
- Vet and interview design candidates
- Set up systems like design critique, async reviews, and component libraries
- Coach and mentor founding designers or juniors

---

## Benefits

- High leverage, low commitment
- Brings senior perspective without long-term cost
- Helps avoid costly hiring mistakes

---

## Limitations

- Not a shipping resource
- Limited hours and availability
- Needs buy-in from the team to be effective

---

## When to Use One

- You're making your first design hire and want to get it right
- You’re unsure how to vet design candidates
- You need short-term leadership before hiring a full-time Head of Design

---

## How to Work With Them

- Set clear goals (e.g., “define hiring rubric” or “vet 3 candidates”)
- Treat them as a partner, not a vendor
- Include them in leadership standups or product reviews
