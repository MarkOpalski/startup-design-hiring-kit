# Founding Designer

**Best for:** Early-stage startups (Pre-seed to Seed) needing hands-on design execution and fast iteration.

---

## Key Traits

- Generalist: Can handle UX, UI, light branding, and user flows
- Comfortable with ambiguity and limited process
- Works closely with founders, PMs, and engineers
- Can prioritize and self-direct without a full design org

---

## Responsibilities

- Design wireframes, mockups, and prototypes in Figma
- Collaborate in product discovery and user research
- Help define early design systems and component libraries
- Advocate for design in product and business decisions

---

## Cautions

- May lack mentorship or growth support
- Risk of burnout if expected to do everything alone
- Needs clear scope and trust to thrive

---

## When to Hire

- You have 1–2 engineers and a PM/founder building the MVP
- You need user experience quickly, not just polished UI
- You're ready to invest in product thinking, not just visuals
